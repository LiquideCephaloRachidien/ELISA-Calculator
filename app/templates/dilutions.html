Reagent dilutions

Mix a concentrated stock down to the working concentration for coating, blocking, wash buffer, or antibodies. The volume to pipette is C1V1 = C2V2, calculated in the browser.

Stocks and targets

Use the same kind of unit for the stock and the target: mg/mL through pg/mL, percent, or X. 1 mg/mL is 1000 µg/mL. A 10X stock brought to 1X in 100 mL takes 10 mL of stock and 90 mL of diluent.
